Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document Type: Veterinary Clinic Cancellation Policy
Applies To: Veterinary appointments booked through participating Petco veterinary clinics

1. Appointment Cancellation

Pet parents may cancel or reschedule a veterinary appointment through the online appointment system or by contacting the clinic directly.

For the best availability, cancellations should be made at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time.

2. Late Cancellations

A cancellation made less than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment may be considered a late cancellation.

Repeated late cancellations may result in restrictions on future appointment scheduling.

3. No-Show Appointments

An appointment is considered a no-show when the pet parent does not arrive for the appointment and does not contact the clinic.

Repeated no-shows may result in additional scheduling restrictions.

4. Cancellation Fees

A cancellation or no-show fee may apply depending on the clinic and appointment type.

If a fee applies, the pet parent will be informed during the appointment booking process or by the clinic.

5. Rescheduling

Pet parents may request to reschedule an appointment, subject to appointment availability.

When possible, the clinic will offer alternative dates and times.

6. Clinic-Initiated Cancellation

If a clinic needs to cancel an appointment because of provider availability, operational issues, or other circumstances, the clinic will attempt to notify the pet parent and provide alternative appointment options.

7. Emergency Situations

For urgent or emergency veterinary situations, pet parents should contact the veterinary clinic directly or seek appropriate emergency veterinary care.

Appointment cancellation procedures should not delay emergency veterinary treatment.

8. Questions About Cancellation

For questions regarding cancellation fees, appointment-specific policies, or clinic requirements, contact the veterinary clinic directly.

FAQ examples for your RAG Agent

Q: How far in advance should I cancel my appointment?
A: Appointments should ideally be cancelled at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment.

Q: Will I be charged for cancelling an appointment?
A: A cancellation fee may apply depending on the clinic and appointment type.

Q: What happens if I don't show up?
A: The appointment may be classified as a no-show. Repeated no-shows may result in scheduling restrictions.

Q: Can I reschedule my appointment?
A: Yes. Rescheduling is possible subject to appointment availability.

Q: What if the clinic cancels my appointment?
A: The clinic will attempt to notify you and provide alternative appointment options.

Q: Does this policy apply to emergencies?
A: Emergency situations should be handled by contacting the clinic directly or seeking appropriate emergency veterinary care. Cancellation procedures should not delay emergency treatment.
